# Evaluation rollout media

The 18 GIFs come from the complete edited clips in `QuickShare_2611100026.zip`. Each plays at 4×, 360×203 pixels, 10 fps, with a 128-color palette and an infinite loop. Matching JPGs provide still states for pause and reduced-motion preferences. `manifest.json` maps each original filename to its location, basket and fruit, and records validated durations, frame counts and sizes.

Both the pitch trial wall and the website Demo evaluation grid use these same GIF/poster pairs. Website clips start playing automatically when visible; Pause clips stops them. The website also provides an enlarged view and suspends clips outside the viewport or while the browser tab is hidden.

`demo-001-silent.mp4` copies the original `demo-001.mp4` video stream and removes its audio track. It retains the original 112-second timeline. The website and pitch slide 9 select 4× through the player; the website also offers 1×.

The separate pitch cover reel is `pitch/assets/rollout-reel.mp4`. It alternates locations across all 18 rollouts, with 6× baked into its approximately 45-second timeline. Its browser playback rate stays at 1×. The original ZIP and videos are preserved.
